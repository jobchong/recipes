# Photography

The photographs were selected from the user-supplied Google Photos album **Food pics for site** on 2026-09-22. The photographer and recipe authors were not identified, so no authorship or tested-recipe claims are made. The album's sharing URL is intentionally not published in the site or repository.

Thirteen photographs illustrate fourteen dish pages. Alternate angles, preparation-only duplicates, and the group portrait were omitted. Dishes only visible as small background sides are represented by the shared-meal pages rather than given speculative recipes. Names and introductory prose are provisional; each page is marked `placeholder: true` until the actual recipe is supplied.

Delivery copies are JPEGs with a longest edge of at most 1200 pixels, compressed for the web. EXIF, IPTC, and comment metadata have been stripped. The photographed food, lighting, and backgrounds have not been generated or retouched. Display framing uses `imagePosition` and optional `imageZoom`; the full photographic composition remains in the local file. Each page declares its file's actual dimensions and supplies descriptive alt text.

The three previous generated example images and their recipe pages have been removed. Images are served locally, with no hotlinks or runtime image service. Fonts remain system fonts; the SVG favicon is the existing letterform mark.
