---
title: Beef and broccoli (牛肉炒西蘭花)
description: Tender marinated beef and crisp broccoli stir-fried in a savoury oyster sauce.
date: '2026-09-02'
totalMinutes: 45
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Beef, Broccoli]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners, Something to share]
source:
  name: Made With Lau
  url: https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/beef-broccoli
ingredients:
  - name: Beef
    items:
      - 12 oz beef, sliced against the grain, not too thin
  - name: Marinade
    items:
      - 1 tsp baking soda
      - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
      - 1 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 2 tbsp water
      - 1 tbsp cornstarch
      - ¼ tsp white pepper
      - 1 tsp sugar
      - 1 tbsp cooking wine
      - 1 tbsp cooking oil, added just before cooking
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
      - 1 tsp light soy sauce
      - 1 tsp dark soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp water
      - 1 tbsp cooking wine
      - 1 tsp sugar
      - 1 tsp cornstarch
      - ¼ tsp white pepper
  - name: Broccoli
    items:
      - 1 lb broccoli, in bite-sized pieces
      - 1 pinch salt and a splash of oil, for the boiling water
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 2 tbsp cooking oil
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 2 garlic cloves, minced
      - 1 small piece ginger, in small cubes
  - name: Vegetables
    items:
      - 1 scallion, cut diagonally
      - ½ carrot, in thin slivers
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 1 tsp sesame oil
steps:
  - Massage the sliced beef in a bowl of water to rinse off the blood, then drain. This keeps it from darkening.
  - Mix the marinade, except the oil. Add the beef, coat it well, and rest 30 minutes.
  - Mix the sauce.
  - Boil the broccoli with the salt and oil for about 2 minutes, then drain.
  - Toss the beef with the 1 tbsp marinade oil.
  - Heat the wok on its highest heat, swirl in the 2 tbsp oil, and pour off the excess into a bowl.
  - Lower the heat. Brown the beef 30–60 seconds, flip, and cook 60–90 seconds until mostly browned. Set it aside to finish later.
  - Add the poured-off oil. Fry the aromatics 20–30 seconds, then the vegetables 20–30 seconds.
  - Add the broccoli for 30–40 seconds, then return the beef for 30–45 seconds.
  - Stir in the sauce for about 1 minute, adding up to 1 tbsp water if it gets dry.
  - Add the sesame oil, toss, and serve.
notes:
  - Pounding the beef with a mallet can replace the baking soda.
  - Cut the carrot thin so it cooks as fast as everything else.
---
The takeout classic made at home. Par-cooking the beef and blanching the broccoli keeps the final stir-fry quick.
