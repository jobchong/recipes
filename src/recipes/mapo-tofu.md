---
title: Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐)
description: Soft tofu and ground pork simmered in a savoury bean and chili sauce.
date: '2026-10-20'
totalMinutes: 20
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Tofu, Pork]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Made With Lau
  url: https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/mapo-tofu-pork
ingredients:
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 1 tbsp oil
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 2 garlic cloves, minced
      - 3 shallots, finely diced
      - 2 dried chili peppers, cut into 6–7 pieces
  - name: Pork
    items:
      - 3 oz ground pork
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - 1 tbsp ground bean sauce
      - 1 cup water
      - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
      - 1 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
      - 1 tsp sugar
      - ½ tsp salt
      - ½ tsp chili sauce
  - name: Tofu
    items:
      - 1 lb soft tofu, cut into 32 cubes
  - name: Slurry
    items:
      - 1½ tbsp cornstarch
      - 2 tbsp water
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 1 stalk green onion, minced – half stirred in, half to garnish
      - 1 mini orange sweet pepper, finely diced
      - 1 mini red sweet pepper, finely diced
      - 1 tsp sesame oil
steps:
  - Drain the tofu without pressing it, and cut it into cubes. Mix the slurry.
  - Heat the wok on high until nearly smoking. Lower the heat, add the oil, swirl, and heat 30–40 seconds.
  - Fry the aromatics 15–20 seconds, until fragrant.
  - Add the pork, break it up, and stir-fry about 1 minute.
  - Add the bean sauce for a few seconds, then the water. Turn the heat to low and add the rest of the sauce.
  - Ease in the tofu gently, since soft tofu breaks. Stir from the bottom to coat.
  - Cover and simmer 1½–2 minutes, then lower the heat and uncover.
  - Pour in the slurry slowly while stirring gently, only as much as needed. Simmer 2 minutes.
  - Stir in half the green onion, the sweet peppers and the sesame oil, then turn off the heat.
  - If it's too thick, thin with water a tablespoon at a time. Garnish with the rest of the green onion.
notes:
  - More dark soy sauce deepens the colour, and more chili sauce makes it redder.
  - Firm or medium tofu works with no other changes.
  - Beef, chicken, shrimp or mushrooms can replace the pork.
---
A milder, Cantonese-leaning mapo tofu. The bean sauce and chili are kept separate, so the heat is easy to adjust.
