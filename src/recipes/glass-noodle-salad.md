---
title: Thai glass noodle salad (ยำวุ้นเส้น)
description: Glass noodles, shrimp, and ground pork tossed with crisp vegetables in a pounded chili-lime dressing.
date: '2026-11-23'
totalMinutes: 35
yield: 2 servings
ingredientTags: [Noodles, Prawns]
occasions: [Cooking for two, Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Hot Thai Kitchen
  url: https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/glass-noodle-salad-v2/
ingredients:
  - name: Noodles
    items:
      - 1.4 oz dry glass noodles (bean threads)
  - name: Dried shrimp
    items:
      - 1 tbsp dried shrimp (optional)
  - name: Dressing
    items:
      - 10 cilantro sprigs – stems in the dressing, leaves to finish
      - 2 garlic cloves
      - 1–3 Thai chilies, to taste
      - 1 tbsp palm sugar, finely chopped and packed
      - 2 tbsp fish sauce
      - 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
  - name: Vegetables
    items:
      - 1 medium tomato, cut into wedges
      - ¼ cup julienned onion
      - 1 stalk Chinese celery or 2 small inner celery stalks with leaves, leaves picked and stalks thinly sliced
  - name: Shrimp
    items:
      - 6 medium or large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  - name: Pork
    items:
      - 3.5 oz ground pork
      - 1 tsp fish sauce
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - ¼ cup unsalted roasted peanuts, roughly chopped
steps:
  - Soak the noodles in room-temperature water 7–10 minutes, until pliable.
  - Drain and snip them into 2–3 sections.
  - Meanwhile, cover the dried shrimp with room-temperature water and microwave on high 45–60 seconds.
  - Rest a few minutes, then lift out the shrimp and pound into bits. Set aside.
  - Halve the cilantro sprigs. Set the leafy tops aside and chop the stems.
  - Pound the cilantro stems, garlic, and chilies into a paste.
  - Pound in the palm sugar until dissolved.
  - Stir in the fish sauce and lime juice.
  - Combine the vegetables and dried shrimp in a large bowl.
  - Boil a medium pot of water and cook the noodles 2 minutes.
  - Lift them into a sieve with tongs, keeping the water boiling.
  - Cook the shrimp in that water about 30 seconds, until done, then add to the bowl.
  - Pour off most of the water, leaving a thin layer, and return to a boil.
  - Add the pork and its fish sauce. Stir until fully cooked.
  - Off the heat, scoop the pork into the bowl with about 1 tbsp cooking liquid.
  - Add the noodles and dressing and toss quickly.
  - Roughly chop the cilantro leaves and toss them in.
  - Plate, top with the peanuts, and serve right away.
notes:
  - Ground chicken or turkey can replace the pork.
  - Any kind of onion works.
  - Choose glass noodles made entirely from mung bean starch if you can.
  - No microwave? Soak the dried shrimp in boiling water for a few minutes instead.
  - No mortar? Chop the softened dried shrimp with a knife.
---
A Thai salad of springy glass noodles, shrimp, and pork, dressed with lime, fish sauce, palm sugar, and pounded chilies.
