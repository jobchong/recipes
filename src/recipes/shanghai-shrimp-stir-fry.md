---
title: Shanghai shrimp stir-fry (油爆虾)
description: Shell-on shrimp flash-fried twice, then tossed in a sweet, tangy reduced sauce.
date: '2026-07-27'
totalMinutes: 25
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Prawns]
occasions: [Something to share]
source:
  name: The Woks of Life
  url: https://thewoksoflife.com/shrimp-stir-fry-shanghai/
ingredients:
  - name: Shrimp
    items:
      - 1 lb (450 g) medium shell-on shrimp (21/30)
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 1 cup oil, for frying
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 2 scallions, white parts only
      - 3 slices ginger
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
      - ½ cup chicken broth
      - 1 tsp sugar
      - ¼ tsp Chinese black vinegar
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 1 tsp sesame oil
      - Salt, to taste
steps:
  - Trim the legs and head tips with scissors, devein through the shell with a toothpick, then rinse and pat dry.
  - Heat the oil in a wok on high until it just smokes.
  - Fry the shrimp in two batches, 5–10 seconds each, until opaque and light pink. Drain.
  - Reheat the oil until smoking and fry both batches again for 5–10 seconds, until deeper pink. Overfrying dries them out.
  - Pour off all but 1 tbsp oil. On low, cook the aromatics 2 minutes.
  - Add the sauce, raise the heat to a simmer, and reduce for 30 seconds.
  - Lift out the aromatics if you like.
  - Add the shrimp and sesame oil, toss 5–10 seconds to coat, salt to taste, and serve hot.
notes:
  - The leftover frying oil tastes of shrimp. Keep it for other stir-fries.
---
A Shanghai favourite. Frying the shrimp twice in very hot oil crisps the shells, and they're eaten shell and all.
