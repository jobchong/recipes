---
title: Pork and pepper stir-fry (辣椒小炒肉)
description: Thin strips of pork stir-fried with blistered long hot green peppers, garlic and ginger.
date: '2026-10-15'
totalMinutes: 20
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Pork, Peppers]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: The Woks of Life
  url: https://thewoksoflife.com/pork-pepper-stir-fry/
ingredients:
  - name: Pork
    items:
      - 8 oz pork shoulder or loin, cut into thin strips
  - name: Marinade
    items:
      - 1 tbsp water
      - 1 tsp cornstarch
      - 2 tsp vegetable oil
      - 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
      - 1 tsp oyster sauce
      - 1 tsp light soy sauce
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 2 tbsp vegetable oil – 1 tbsp for the pork, 1 tbsp for the peppers
  - name: Peppers and aromatics
    items:
      - 6 long hot green peppers (10–12 oz / 280–340 g), seeded and thinly sliced on the diagonal
      - 2 slices ginger
      - 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  - name: Deglaze
    items:
      - 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - ½ tsp sugar
      - ½ tsp dark soy sauce
steps:
  - Mix the pork with the marinade and set it aside.
  - Heat the wok on high until smoking. Add 1 tbsp oil and stir-fry the pork 2–3 minutes, until browned at the edges. Set it aside.
  - On medium-high, add the rest of the oil with the peppers and aromatics. Fry 30–60 seconds until the peppers blister, without burning the garlic.
  - Splash in the wine and stir 10 seconds.
  - Return the pork with the sugar and dark soy sauce, toss a few seconds on high, and serve.
notes:
  - For less heat, use cubanelle or Anaheim peppers.
  - Spoon it over noodles in broth for a noodle-shop meal.
---
A fast Hunan-style stir-fry. The peppers are the star, and the pork is there to season them.
