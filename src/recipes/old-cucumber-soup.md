---
title: Old cucumber soup (老黄瓜汤)
description: A slow-simmered clear soup of pork bones, old cucumber, dried cuttlefish, red dates and wolfberries.
date: '2026-08-28'
totalMinutes: 155
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Cucumber, Pork]
occasions: [Soups and drinks, Slow weekends]
source:
  name: The Burning Kitchen
  url: https://theburningkitchen.com/old-cucumber-soup/
ingredients:
  - name: Bones
    items:
      - 500 g pork bones
      - 2 chicken carcasses
  - name: Soup pot
    items:
      - 1 medium old cucumber
      - 1 dried cuttlefish
      - 3 tbsp Chinese peanuts, skins on, washed
      - 2.5 litres water
  - name: Last 15 minutes
    items:
      - 6–8 red dates, pitted and rinsed
      - 2 tbsp wolfberries, rinsed
  - name: To season
    items:
      - 1 pinch salt (optional)
steps:
  - Blanch the bones 3 minutes, then drain, rinse, and pick off any clots.
  - Blanch the cuttlefish 1 minute. Remove the bone, mouth and eyes, and cut it into 1-inch pieces.
  - Halve the cucumber lengthwise, scrape out the seeds, and cut it into 2-inch pieces.
  - Boil the water on high, then add the bones, cuttlefish, cucumber and peanuts.
  - When it boils again, simmer on medium for 2 hours.
  - Add the dates and wolfberries and simmer 15 minutes more. Longer turns the soup sour.
  - Salt to taste and serve.
notes:
  - Top up with boiling water, not cold.
  - To remove the fat, chill the soup overnight and lift it off, or use a fat separator.
  - Don't soak the cuttlefish or the wolfberries.
  - Cured Chinese ham can replace the cuttlefish. For a nut-free soup, leave out the peanuts.
---
A Cantonese-style home soup. The old cucumber cooks down soft and mild, and the dried cuttlefish adds a gentle sweetness.
