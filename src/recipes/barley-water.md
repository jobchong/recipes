---
title: Barley water (薏米水)
description: A lightly sweet barley drink simmered with candied winter melon and pandan, served warm or chilled.
date: '2026-07-26'
totalMinutes: 44
yield: 4–6 servings
ingredientTags: [Barley]
occasions: [Soups and drinks]
source:
  name: Noob Cook
  url: https://noobcook.com/barley-water/
ingredients:
  - name: Pot
    items:
      - 150 g Holland barley, washed and drained
      - 100 g candied winter melon
      - 4 pandan leaves, tied in a knot (optional)
      - 1.5 litres water, plus more to top up
steps:
  - Put everything in a large pot and bring to a boil.
  - Lower to a simmer with the lid slightly open.
  - Simmer about 40 minutes, topping up the water as needed, until the barley is soft and the water turns partly milky.
  - Taste. If it needs more sweetness, add more winter melon or some rock sugar in the last 10 minutes.
  - Serve warm or chilled, with or without the barley.
notes:
  - Chinese barley needs 30 minutes or more, or a 1–2 hour soak in hot water first.
  - To use rock sugar instead, add about 50 g in the last 5 minutes.
  - If you won't eat the barley, 15–20 minutes is enough. The drink will be thinner.
  - Keeps 2–3 days in the fridge. Leftover barley can go into soups and stews.
---
A simple, cooling drink. The barley softens slowly and turns the water cloudy and gently sweet.
