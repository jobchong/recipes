---
title: Braised pork with potatoes (薯仔炆豬肉)
description: Pork belly and potatoes braised in a savoury bean and oyster sauce until tender.
date: '2026-11-12'
totalMinutes: 40
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Pork, Potatoes]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Made With Lau
  url: https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/braised-pork-with-potatoes
ingredients:
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 3 tsp oil – 1 tsp per batch
  - name: Onion
    items:
      - 3 oz red onion, in bite-sized pieces
  - name: Potatoes
    items:
      - 2 lb potatoes, peeled and cut into bite-sized triangles
  - name: Pork
    items:
      - 1 lb pork belly, cut into pieces at least 1 inch thick
  - name: Garlic
    items:
      - 3 garlic cloves, each cut in thirds
  - name: Seasoning
    items:
      - 1 tbsp ground bean sauce
      - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
      - 1 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 2 tsp sugar
  - name: Carrot
    items:
      - 3 oz carrot, cut into triangles
  - name: Braise
    items:
      - 3 cups boiling water
      - ½ tsp salt, to taste
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 3 stalks green onion, green parts only, in 2-inch batons
      - 1 tsp sesame oil
steps:
  - Keep the cut potatoes in cold water until needed, then drain.
  - Scrape the pork skin with a knife under cold water. Pat dry and cut.
  - Heat the wok on high. Stir-fry the onion in 1 tsp oil about 30 seconds and set aside.
  - Add 1 tsp oil and stir-fry the potatoes about 2 minutes until golden. Set aside, since they finish cooking in the braise.
  - Add the last 1 tsp oil and the pork. Brown 30–40 seconds a side.
  - Add the garlic and stir-fry about 20 seconds.
  - Turn the heat to its lowest. Add the bean sauce, oyster sauce and soy sauce, and stir briefly.
  - On medium, stir in the sugar, then the potatoes and carrot, for 30 seconds.
  - Pour in the boiling water to almost cover. Cover and cook on medium-high 5 minutes.
  - Taste and add salt. Cover and cook on medium until the potatoes are tender, about 3–4 minutes, adding water if it gets dry.
  - Off the heat, stir in the onion, green onion and sesame oil. Serve over rice.
notes:
  - Pork butt can replace the belly. Avoid lean cuts like chops, which dry out.
  - Keeps in the fridge for up to a week and tastes better the next day. Don't freeze it, which ruins the potatoes' texture.
---
A Cantonese home-style braise. The potatoes soak up the sauce, which is meant to go over plenty of rice.
