---
title: Four-ingredient okra stir-fry
description: Blanched okra stir-fried in Sichuan peppercorn oil with dried chilies and a splash of soy.
date: '2026-07-30'
totalMinutes: 20
yield: 2 servings
ingredientTags: [Okra]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners, Cooking for two]
source:
  name: Omnivore's Cookbook
  url: https://omnivorescookbook.com/four-ingredient-okra-stir-fry/
ingredients:
  - name: Okra
    items:
      - ½ lb okra
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 1 tbsp peanut or vegetable oil
      - 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  - name: Chili
    items:
      - 2 dried chili peppers, broken into 2–3 pieces
  - name: Seasoning
    items:
      - 2 tsp light soy sauce
steps:
  - Boil the okra 30 seconds. Drain, rinse under cold water, and drain well.
  - Slice it on the bias into ½-inch (1 cm) pieces.
  - Heat the oil and peppercorns over medium until the peppercorns darken and smell fragrant.
  - Turn the heat to its lowest setting and scoop out the peppercorns.
  - Add the chilies and stir a few times.
  - On medium-high, add the okra and stir about 1 minute to coat.
  - Swirl in the soy sauce and keep stirring until the okra is tender but still a little chewy.
  - Taste, add salt if needed, and serve hot.
notes:
  - Use tamari instead of soy sauce for a gluten-free version.
  - Leave the chilies whole for a milder dish.
---
A minimal side dish. A quick blanch keeps the okra from turning slimy, and the peppercorn oil gives it a gentle tingle.
