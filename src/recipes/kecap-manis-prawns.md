---
title: Kecap manis prawns
description: Deep-fried prawns tossed with a whole bulb of garlic, spring onions, kecap manis and Shaoxing wine.
date: '2026-11-28'
totalMinutes: 25
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Prawns, Spring onions]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Spice N' Pans
  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ef9V3DLsT2M
ingredients:
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - Oil, for deep-frying – 1–2 tbsp kept for the sauce
  - name: Prawns
    items:
      - 600 g (21.2 oz) prawns or shrimp, deveined, eyes snipped off to stop splattering
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 1 bulb garlic, chopped
      - 6 stalks spring onion – white stems first, the rest to finish
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - 80 ml (2.7 fl oz) kecap manis (Indonesian sweet soy sauce)
      - 2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing huadiao)
steps:
  - Heat the oil in a wok over medium-high until very hot.
  - Test with a chopstick – bubbles should rise from it rapidly.
  - Slide the prawns in carefully and spread them out.
  - Deep-fry briefly, just until cooked through.
  - Lift out every prawn before turning off the heat, so they don't absorb oil.
  - Pour off all but 1–2 tbsp of the oil.
  - Over medium heat, add the garlic, then the spring onion stems.
  - Stir-fry until fragrant, without letting the garlic burn.
  - Return the prawns and toss well.
  - Add the kecap manis and mix well.
  - Splash the wine around the edge of the wok.
  - Toss in the rest of the spring onions, mix, and take off the heat.
notes:
  - To skip deep-frying, pan-fry the prawns in some oil over medium-high heat. Cook them before the garlic goes in either way, or the garlic burns before the prawns are done.
---
Prawns fried in their shells, then tossed with a whole bulb of garlic, spring onions and kecap manis, Indonesia's sweet soy sauce. Spice N' Pans call it garlic soy prawns. It needs only five ingredients.
