title: Roasted tomatoes & white beans
description: Jammy tomatoes and creamy beans, best eaten with a good piece of bread.

totalMinutes: 40
yield: 2 generous servings

imageAlt: Roasted cherry tomatoes and white beans with basil in a shallow ceramic baking dish.
ingredientTags: [Tomatoes, Beans, Garlic]
occasions: [Slow weekends, Weeknight dinners]
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 500 g cherry tomatoes
      - 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
      - 3 tbsp olive oil
      - ½ tsp fine salt, plus more to taste
      - Freshly ground black pepper
      - 2 × 400 g tins white beans, drained and rinsed
      - 60 ml water
      - 1 tsp red wine vinegar, plus more to taste
      - A small handful of basil leaves
      - Crusty bread, to serve
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Put the tomatoes and garlic in a medium baking dish. Toss with the olive oil, salt, and a few grinds of black pepper.
  - Roast for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have begun to burst and release their juices.
  - Stir in the beans and water, gently crushing a few tomatoes with the back of a spoon. Return to the oven for 10–15 minutes, until the beans are hot and the edges are bubbling.
  - Stir in the vinegar. Taste and adjust the salt and acidity. Tear the basil over the top and serve warm with bread to catch the juices.
notes:
  - Cannellini, butter beans, or haricot beans all work. Drain them well so the sauce stays rich.
  - Use a dish that holds everything fairly snugly. A very wide tray lets the tomato juices evaporate too quickly.
  - Refrigerate leftovers promptly in a covered container for up to 3 days. Reheat with a splash of water until piping hot.
---
The oven does most of the work here. Tomatoes collapse into olive oil and garlic; a couple of tins of beans turn their juices into something you’ll want to mop up with bread.

It’s an easy supper as it stands, or something to put in the middle of the table alongside a sharp green salad.
