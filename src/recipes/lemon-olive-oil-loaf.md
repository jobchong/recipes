date: 2026-09-19
example: true
totalMinutes: 75
yield: 1 loaf (8–10 slices)
image: /assets/images/lemon-olive-oil-loaf.jpg

ingredientTags: [Lemon, Olive oil, Eggs]
occasions: [Afternoon tea, Something to share]
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 200 g plain flour
      - 1½ tsp baking powder
      - ¼ tsp fine salt
      - 170 g caster sugar
      - Finely grated zest of 2 lemons
      - 3 large eggs, at room temperature
      - 120 ml mild olive oil
      - 100 g plain yoghurt
      - 2 tbsp lemon juice
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease a 900 g / 2 lb loaf tin, approximately 23 × 13 cm, and line with baking paper, leaving an overhang on the long sides.
  - Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl. In a separate large bowl, rub the sugar and lemon zest together with your fingertips until fragrant.
  - Add the eggs to the lemon sugar and whisk for 1–2 minutes, until well combined and slightly thickened. Whisk in the olive oil, then the yoghurt and lemon juice.
  - Fold in the dry ingredients just until no flour remains. Scrape into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
  - Bake for 45–55 minutes, until golden and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean. If the top is colouring too quickly, cover loosely with foil for the final part of baking.
  - Leave in the tin for 15 minutes, then lift onto a wire rack. Let the loaf cool completely before slicing. The total time above excludes this final cooling time.
notes:
  - Choose an olive oil you enjoy tasting, but save particularly peppery oils for another use; a mild, fruity one is lovely here.
  - Rubbing the zest into the sugar releases the lemon oils and makes the whole cake more fragrant.
  - Keep the cooled loaf in an airtight container for up to 3 days, or freeze individual slices for up to 2 months.
---
A simple loaf cake is a very good thing to have around. This one has olive oil for a tender crumb, yoghurt for a little tang, and enough lemon zest to make itself known.

There’s no icing to make and no mixer to wash. Just let it cool, cut a thick slice, and put the kettle on.
