---
title: Gyudon
description: Thinly sliced beef and onion simmered in a sweet dashi and soy sauce, spooned over rice.
date: '2026-08-27'
totalMinutes: 20
yield: 2 servings
ingredientTags: [Beef, Rice]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners, Cooking for two]
source:
  name: Just One Cookbook
  url: https://www.justonecookbook.com/gyudon/
ingredients:
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - ½ cup dashi
      - 2 tbsp sake
      - 2 tbsp mirin
      - 3 tbsp soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp sugar
  - name: Beef and onion
    items:
      - ½ yellow onion (4 oz / 113 g), thinly sliced
      - ½ lb thinly sliced beef, such as ribeye, cut into 3-inch pieces
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 1 green onion, thinly sliced on the diagonal
  - name: To serve
    items:
      - 2 bowls cooked Japanese short-grain rice
      - Pickled red ginger
steps:
  - Cook the rice. If you're slicing your own beef, freeze it for 10 minutes first.
  - In a cold frying pan, stir the sauce until the sugar dissolves.
  - Spread the onion through the liquid and lay the beef over it.
  - Cover and turn the heat to medium. Once it simmers, lower the heat and cook 3–4 minutes, skimming once or twice.
  - Scatter the green onion over, cover, and cook 1 minute more.
  - Spoon a little sauce over each bowl of rice, top with the beef and onion, and garnish with pickled ginger.
notes:
  - Swap the sake for dry sherry or water. Swap the mirin for 2 tbsp sake or water plus 2 tsp sugar.
  - Leftovers keep 2–3 days in the fridge or 3–4 weeks in the freezer. Reheat gently in the sauce.
  - For an egg version, pour in beaten egg along with the green onion.
---
A Japanese weeknight staple. Starting everything in a cold pan keeps the thin beef tender.
