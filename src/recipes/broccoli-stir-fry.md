---
title: Broccoli stir-fry (炒西蘭花)
description: Blanched broccoli stir-fried with garlic and ginger in a light, glossy sauce.
date: '2026-07-16'
totalMinutes: 20
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Broccoli]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Made With Lau
  url: https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/broccoli-stir-fry
ingredients:
  - name: Broccoli
    items:
      - 1 lb broccoli (about 2 heads), cut into small florets, stems peeled and thinly sliced
      - 1 tsp salt, for soaking
  - name: Blanching water
    items:
      - 1 tsp salt
      - 1 tsp oil
      - Ice water, enough to cover the broccoli
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 1 oz garlic (4–5 cloves), finely minced
      - ½ oz ginger, smashed and minced
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 2 tbsp oil – 1 tbsp to start, 1 tbsp to finish
  - name: Wine
    items:
      - 1 tsp cooking wine
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - ½ tsp salt
      - 1 tsp sugar
      - 1 tbsp cooking wine
      - 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder
      - 2 tsp cornstarch
      - 2 tbsp water
      - 1 tsp oyster sauce
steps:
  - Soak the whole broccoli in cold water with the salt for 3–5 minutes, swishing, then cut it up.
  - Mix the sauce.
  - Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil with the blanching salt and oil.
  - Add the stems, then the florets. Cover and cook until it boils again, about 3 minutes.
  - Move the broccoli to the ice water for 1 minute, then drain well. Dry the wok.
  - Heat the dry wok on high, lower the heat, and add 1 tbsp oil.
  - On medium, fry the garlic 15–20 seconds, then the ginger 20–30 seconds.
  - Turn the heat to high, add the broccoli, and stir-fry 40–50 seconds.
  - Drizzle the wine around the edge of the wok.
  - Stir the sauce and add it a little at a time, only as much as needed, stir-frying about 1½ minutes.
  - Add the last 1 tbsp oil, toss 15–20 seconds, and turn off the heat.
notes:
  - Frozen broccoli works in a pinch. Add it straight from frozen.
  - Plate with the stems at the bottom and scrape all the sauce over.
---
Restaurant-style broccoli. Blanching keeps it bright green, and the stir-fry stays brief so it stays crisp.
