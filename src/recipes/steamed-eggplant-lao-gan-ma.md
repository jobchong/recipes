---
title: Steamed Chinese eggplant with Lao Gan Ma
description: Silky steamed eggplant strips under a tangy soy and chili sauce, finished with sizzling garlic oil.
date: '2026-07-20'
totalMinutes: 25
yield: 6 servings
ingredientTags: [Eggplant]
occasions: [Something to share]
source:
  name: The Woks of Life
  url: https://thewoksoflife.com/steamed-chinese-eggplant-spicy/
ingredients:
  - name: Eggplant
    items:
      - 1 lb (450 g) Japanese or Chinese eggplant (about 3)
  - name: Soak
    items:
      - 2 quarts water
      - ¼ cup white vinegar
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - 1 tsp Chinese black vinegar
      - 2 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 2 tsp oyster sauce
      - ½ tsp sugar
      - 1 tsp sesame oil
      - 1–1½ tbsp Lao Gan Ma chili sauce
  - name: Topping
    items:
      - 4 garlic cloves, minced
      - 1 scallion, minced
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 3 tbsp vegetable oil
steps:
  - Start the steamer on low to preheat.
  - Cut each eggplant crosswise into 3 pieces, then each piece into 8–10 strips.
  - Soak the strips in the vinegar water for 3 minutes. Drain and squeeze dry.
  - Spread them on a rimmed heatproof dish, cover, and steam on high 8–10 minutes.
  - Meanwhile, mix the sauce.
  - Lift out the dish, keeping its juices, and pour the sauce over the eggplant.
  - Arrange the garlic and scallion on top in neat lines.
  - Heat the oil until it smokes lightly and pour it over the topping so it sizzles.
notes:
  - The vinegar soak keeps the eggplant purple after steaming.
  - Any chili sauce or chili oil can stand in for Lao Gan Ma.
  - Serve hot, or make it ahead and serve cold.
---
A quick steamed vegetable dish. The eggplant soaks up a sharp, spicy dressing, and it's just as good cold the next day.
