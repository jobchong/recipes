---
title: Nyonya chap chye
description: Cabbage, mushrooms, lily buds and bean curd skin simmered in prawn stock with garlic, pork belly and tau cheo.
date: '2026-11-28'
totalMinutes: 120
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Cabbage, Mushrooms, Pork, Prawns]
occasions: [Something to share, Slow weekends]
source:
  name: Malcolm Lee, The Singapore Women's Weekly
  url: https://www.womensweekly.com.sg/food/chap-chye
ingredients:
  - name: Mushrooms
    items:
      - 25 g black mushrooms
  - name: Lily buds and wood ear
    items:
      - 10 g dried lily buds (kim chiam), knotted so they don't fray
      - 15 g wood ear fungus
  - name: Glass noodles
    items:
      - 5 g glass noodles (tang hoon)
  - name: Prawns and stock
    items:
      - 100 g prawns
      - 200 ml water
  - name: Bean curd
    items:
      - Vegetable oil, for deep-frying
      - 15 g dried yellow bean curd skin (tau kee), snipped into small pieces
      - 1 piece sweet bean curd sheet (tiam tau kee), cut into strips
  - name: Pork
    items:
      - 90 g pork belly
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 2 tbsp vegetable oil
      - 15 g garlic, chopped
  - name: Tau cheo
    items:
      - 1 tbsp fermented soy bean paste (tau cheo)
  - name: Soy sauces
    items:
      - 1 tsp dark soy sauce
      - 1 tsp light soy sauce
  - name: Cabbage
    items:
      - 100 g cabbage leaves, cut into 5 cm squares
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - Salt and sugar, to taste
      - Steamed rice, to serve
steps:
  - Soak the mushrooms, lily buds and wood ear in separate bowls of hot water.
  - Soak the glass noodles in cold water – hot water makes them too soft.
  - Peel and devein the prawns, keeping the shells.
  - Dry-roast the shells in a pot until fragrant.
  - Add the water and simmer on low for about 30 minutes.
  - Meanwhile, drain the mushrooms, lily buds and wood ear once softened.
  - Stem the mushrooms, squeeze them dry and slice into strips.
  - Trim the hard underside off the wood ear; cut the rest into smaller pieces.
  - Snip the hard ends off the lily buds.
  - Deep-fry both bean curds gently until golden brown, then drain.
  - Blanch the pork belly in boiling water for 5 minutes.
  - Let the pork cool, then slice into 1 cm strips.
  - Strain the prawn stock and set aside.
  - Heat the 2 tbsp oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  - Fry the garlic until fragrant.
  - Add the mushrooms and pork; fry 1 minute.
  - Add the tau cheo; fry 1 minute.
  - Add the prawn stock, soy sauces, lily buds, wood ear, glass noodles and bean curd.
  - Simmer 20 minutes.
  - Add the cabbage and simmer 10 minutes more.
  - Add the prawns and cook 2 minutes.
  - Season with salt and sugar, and serve with steamed rice.
---
A Nyonya braise of cabbage, black mushrooms, lily buds, wood ear and fried bean curd skin, simmered in a stock made from the prawn shells and seasoned with garlic and tau cheo. The recipe is by Malcolm Lee, published in The Singapore Women's Weekly.
