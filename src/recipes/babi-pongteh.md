---
title: Babi pongteh
description: Nyonya pork belly braised with taucheo, dried mushrooms and potatoes until the sauce cooks down and clings.
date: '2026-11-28'
totalMinutes: 200
yield: 8 servings
ingredientTags: [Pork, Potatoes, Mushrooms]
occasions: [Something to share, Slow weekends]
source:
  name: Singapore Noodles
  url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nIoRGwHb2ts
ingredients:
  - name: Pork
    items:
      - 1½ kg pork belly, cut into cubes
      - 4 tsp dark soy sauce
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 40 g oil
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 50 g red onions, chopped
      - 80 g garlic, chopped
  - name: Taucheo
    items:
      - 60 g taucheo (fermented soybean paste)
  - name: Braise
    items:
      - 750 g water
      - 60 g Chinese dried mushrooms
      - 2 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 2 tsp dark soy sauce
      - 20 g coconut sugar
      - ¼ tsp salt, to taste
  - name: Potatoes
    items:
      - Potatoes, cut to about the size of the pork – optional; the source gives no amount
  - name: To serve
    items:
      - Coriander
      - Crushed chillies
steps:
  - Put the pork in boiling water for a minute or so, then drain and discard the water.
  - Rinse the pork well, drain and move to a large bowl.
  - Toss with the 4 tsp dark soy sauce and leave about 30 minutes.
  - Heat the oil and fry the onions until lightly browned.
  - Add the garlic and sauté briefly – don't let it burn.
  - Add the taucheo and fry 1 minute, until fragrant.
  - Add the pork and the braise ingredients.
  - Taste – it should be slightly under-seasoned, since the liquid reduces.
  - Cover and simmer 2 hours, until the pork is tender but still has some bite.
  - Add the potatoes, cover and simmer 30 minutes more, until tender.
  - Turn the heat to high and stir until the sauce reduces and coats everything.
  - Scatter over the coriander and crushed chillies.
notes:
  - Pig trotters are the traditional cut; pork belly is used here as it's easier to find and stays moist.
  - The mushrooms go in dry. You can soak them and remove the stalks first if you prefer.
  - The potatoes are optional.
---
A Nyonya braise of pork belly in taucheo (fermented soybean paste), seasoned with soy and coconut sugar and reduced at the end so the sauce coats the meat. Singapore Noodles adapted it from the cookbook *Cooking for the President*; eat it with plenty of white rice.
