---
title: Japanese potato salad
description: Lightly mashed russets with carrot, corn, cucumber, egg and ham, folded through Kewpie mayonnaise.
date: '2026-11-15'
totalMinutes: 80
yield: 6 servings
ingredientTags: [Potatoes, Eggs, Carrots]
occasions: [Something to share]
source:
  name: Just One Cookbook
  url: https://www.justonecookbook.com/japanese-potato-salad/
ingredients:
  - name: Potatoes
    items:
      - 2 russet potatoes (1.15 lb / 520 g), peeled and cut into 1½-inch pieces
      - 2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (half for table salt)
  - name: To season the potatoes
    items:
      - 1 Tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar
      - Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  - name: Carrot and corn
    items:
      - 2 inches carrot (2 oz / 60 g), peeled, halved or quartered lengthwise and thinly sliced
      - ¼ cup frozen or canned corn
  - name: Egg
    items:
      - 1 large egg
  - name: Cucumber
    items:
      - 1 Persian cucumber (or ½ Japanese cucumber; 3 oz / 90 g), partly peeled in strips and thinly sliced
      - ½ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (half for table salt)
  - name: Ham
    items:
      - 2 slices Black Forest ham (1.9 oz / 54 g), cut into thin 1-inch strips
  - name: Mayonnaise
    items:
      - 6 Tbsp Kewpie mayonnaise – 4 Tbsp first, the rest to taste
steps:
  - Cover the potatoes with cold water by 1 inch; add the salt.
  - Heat over medium-high, lid slightly ajar, until boiling.
  - Uncover, drop to medium and boil gently about 15 minutes, until a skewer slides through.
  - Drain well, then shake the pot over medium heat until dry.
  - Mash roughly, leaving small chunks, and move to a large bowl.
  - Toss the hot potatoes with the vinegar and pepper, then leave to cool.
  - Hold the mayo until cool – it separates in hot potatoes.
  - Meanwhile, boil the carrot and frozen corn 5 minutes (canned corn needs none).
  - Drain the carrot and corn in a fine sieve and let cool.
  - Put the egg in the same pot, cover with cold water by 1 inch.
  - Bring to a boil over medium heat, then simmer gently 11–12 minutes.
  - Cool the egg in cold water, peel and chop finely.
  - Rub the cucumber with its salt and rest 5 minutes.
  - Squeeze the cucumber completely dry.
  - Add the vegetables, egg and ham to the cooled potatoes and mix.
  - Stir in 4 Tbsp mayo; add the rest or a pinch of salt to taste.
  - Chill 30–60 minutes, or serve straight away.
notes:
  - Stick with starchy russets; waxy potatoes like Yukon Gold don't mash the same way.
  - Lemon juice (½ Tbsp) or apple cider, Champagne or white wine vinegar can stand in for rice vinegar.
  - No Kewpie? Mix 1 cup regular mayonnaise with 2 Tbsp rice vinegar and 1 Tbsp sugar.
  - Leave out the ham for a meatless version, or use tuna instead.
  - The carrot can be microwaved until just tender instead of boiled.
  - Make it at least 4 hours or up to a day ahead; squeeze the cucumber well so it doesn't water down.
  - Keeps 3–4 days in an airtight container in the fridge.
---
A creamy, chunky Japanese-style potato salad: the potatoes are seasoned with rice vinegar while warm, then folded with crisp vegetables, chopped egg and ham once cool.
