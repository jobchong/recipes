---
title: Three cup chicken (三杯鸡)
description: Chicken thighs braised with sesame oil, Shaoxing wine and soy, then finished with Thai basil.
date: '2026-08-25'
totalMinutes: 25
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Chicken]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Red House Spice
  url: https://redhousespice.com/three-cup-chicken/
ingredients:
  - name: Aromatics
    items:
      - 3½ tbsp sesame oil – 3 tbsp to start, ½ tbsp to finish
      - 20 slices ginger
      - 20 garlic cloves, whole or halved
      - 3 scallions – whites here, greens with the pepper
  - name: Chicken
    items:
      - 2 lb chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized chunks
  - name: Sauce
    items:
      - ½ cup Shaoxing wine
      - 2 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp brown sugar
      - ¼ tsp ground white pepper
  - name: Pepper
    items:
      - 1 bell pepper, cut into chunks
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 1 handful fresh Thai basil
steps:
  - Fry the ginger, garlic and scallion whites in 3 tbsp sesame oil on low, until fragrant and the garlic browns slightly.
  - Push them aside, add the chicken in one layer, and sear on medium, turning, until it has mostly lost its pinkness.
  - Add the sauce, toss, cover, and braise on high about 5 minutes.
  - Uncover, stir in the pepper and scallion greens, and keep cooking on high.
  - When the liquid has reduced as much as you like, turn off the heat.
  - Stir in the remaining sesame oil and the basil. Serve with rice.
notes:
  - Braise wings for about 8 minutes, drumsticks 12, or breast just 3.
  - Sweet basil can replace Thai basil, or skip it and use more scallions.
  - If your Shaoxing wine is salty, use less and make up the volume with water.
  - The more the sauce reduces, the saltier and thicker it gets.
---
A Taiwanese favourite, named for its traditional equal measures of sesame oil, rice wine and soy sauce.
