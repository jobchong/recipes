ingredients:
  - name: For the noodles
    items:
      - 200 g dried wheat noodles
      - 6 spring onions, thinly sliced, whites and greens separated
      - 25 g fresh ginger, peeled and finely grated
      - 3 tbsp neutral oil
      - 2 tbsp light soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp rice vinegar
      - 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
      - ½ tsp sugar
      - Chilli oil, to serve (optional)
steps:
  - Bring a large pot of water to the boil. Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions. Reserve a mug of cooking water, then drain.
  - While the noodles cook, heat the neutral oil in a large frying pan over medium-low heat. Add the ginger and spring onion whites. Cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring, until fragrant but not browned.
  - Stir in the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, sugar, and 2 tablespoons of the noodle water. Add the drained noodles and toss over low heat until glossy, loosening with another splash of water if needed.
  - Fold in most of the spring onion greens. Divide between two bowls and finish with the remaining greens and chilli oil, if you like. Serve straight away.
notes:
  - Have the ginger and spring onions ready before you begin. Once the noodles are cooked, everything comes together quickly.
  - Wheat noodles vary in saltiness. Taste before adding more soy sauce, especially if your noodles are already salted.
  - A fried egg, crisp tofu, or a handful of greens makes this a more substantial meal.
---
Some evenings call for very little between you and a bowl of noodles. Here, ginger and spring onions soften in warm oil while the water comes to a boil. A splash of soy, a little vinegar, and dinner is nearly there.

Keep the heat gentle. You want the ginger fragrant and the onions sweet, with plenty of fresh green folded through at the end.
