---
title: Tomato and eggs (番茄炒蛋)
description: Soft scrambled eggs folded into sweet, saucy stir-fried tomatoes.
date: '2026-07-30'
totalMinutes: 15
yield: 4 servings
ingredientTags: [Tomatoes, Eggs]
occasions: [Weeknight dinners]
source:
  name: Made With Lau
  url: https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/tomato-and-eggs
ingredients:
  - name: Tomatoes
    items:
      - 1½ lb ripe medium tomatoes
  - name: Eggs
    items:
      - 4 eggs
      - ½ tsp salt
  - name: Oil
    items:
      - 2 tbsp oil, plus a little more for the tomatoes
  - name: Ginger
    items:
      - 5 slices ginger, cut into thin strips
  - name: Seasoning
    items:
      - 3 tbsp water
      - ½ tsp salt
      - 2 tbsp brown sugar
  - name: Slurry
    items:
      - 1 tbsp cornstarch
      - 4 tbsp water
  - name: To finish
    items:
      - 2 tbsp ketchup
steps:
  - To peel (optional), score the tomatoes, cover them with boiling water for 2 minutes, then slip off the skins.
  - Halve the tomatoes, cut out the hard core, and cut each half into 4 wedges.
  - Beat the eggs with the salt until frothy. Mix the slurry.
  - Heat the pan on high, add the oil, and gently scramble the eggs until about 80% set, around 35 seconds. Set aside.
  - Add a little more oil and the ginger, then the tomatoes. Stir-fry 30 seconds.
  - Add the seasoning and cook about 2½ minutes, covering the pan if it won't hold a boil.
  - Stir the slurry and pour it in slowly, only until the sauce is as thick as you like.
  - Return the eggs and stir about 50 seconds.
  - Off the heat, stir in the ketchup. Taste and serve.
notes:
  - Peeled tomatoes give a saucier dish, and unpeeled ones stay chunkier.
  - Add more sugar if the tomatoes are sour.
  - Keep the heat high and don't cut back the oil, which is what makes the eggs fluffy.
---
Home-style comfort food. The eggs come out of the pan early so they stay soft when they go back into the sauce.
